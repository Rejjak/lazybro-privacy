Privacy Policy

LazyBro Poster · Last updated 16 November 2026

In short: LazyBro Poster only publishes our own videos to the LazyBro Facebook Page. It does not collect, store or share any information about you.

About this app

LazyBro Poster is a private tool used only by the owner of the LazyBro Facebook Page. It publishes short educational and entertainment videos to that Page.

What data we collect

None. The app does not collect, store or share personal data from Facebook users, Page visitors or followers. It does not read comments, messages, reactions or profile information.

How the app uses Facebook

The app uses the Facebook Graph API only to upload videos and their captions to the LazyBro Page, on behalf of the Page owner. The access token it uses belongs to the Page owner and is stored privately.

Services we use

Our videos are created with these tools. None of your data is sent to them.

  • Google Gemini, to write the video scripts
  • Microsoft Edge text-to-speech, for voiceovers
  • Pixabay, for free stock footage

Data deletion

Because the app stores no data about you, there is nothing to delete. If you have a question or request, send a message to the LazyBro Facebook Page and we will reply within 30 days.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date at the top.

Contact

Send a message to the LazyBro Facebook Page.
